"Chapter 08 — Questions people ask Jack

Straight answers.

Is the 15-minute call really free?

Yes. There is no fee and no obligation. Independent agents are compensated by the insurance company only if you choose to put a policy in force.

Do I have to meet in person?

No. Most conversations happen by phone or video. Lionel serves clients across Texas and meets in person in the Houston / Clear Lake / Galveston County area on request.

I have coverage through work. Isn't that enough?

Group coverage is a great start, but it usually ends when the job does and is often 1–2× salary. A quick review shows whether there's a gap worth closing.

Can you help with auto and home too?

Yes — Jack Auto, Jack Home and Jack Commercial quotes are shopped across multiple carriers. Send a quick quote request and we'll tell you what we need.

Are cash-value and retirement strategies guaranteed?

Guarantees are provided by the issuing insurance company and depend on its claims-paying ability. Lionel will show you the guaranteed and non-guaranteed columns of any illustration side by side.
