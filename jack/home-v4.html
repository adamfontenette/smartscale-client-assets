Is the 15-minute call really free?

Yes. No fee, no obligation. I'm compensated by the insurance company only if you choose to put a policy in force.

Do I have to meet in person?

No. Most conversations happen by phone or video. I serve clients across Texas and meet in person in the Houston / Clear Lake / Galveston County area on request.
